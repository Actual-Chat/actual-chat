**🎉 Voxt v2.22 is here! 🎉**

Voice and video calls are here! Plus, connect your AI agent to Voxt, bring over chat history from another messenger, and explore our new public documentation.

**📞 Introducing voice & video calls**
- Call someone directly in Voxt, with incoming-call alerts and clear ringing and connection status
- Turn on your camera or share your screen, go full-screen, and keep video in sight while switching chats — or dock it beside your conversation on a wide screen
- Raise your hand and react with emoji in calls and live conversations; group video supports up to 15 cameras
- Choose between the earpiece, speaker and headset on supported mobile devices
- Find calls in your chat history, with their duration and why they ended

**🤖 Your AI agent, connected**
- Agent MCP lets your AI assistant work with your Voxt messages: find conversations, read messages, and send replies on your behalf
- Bring existing chat history from another messenger into Voxt with your agent's help — each member gives consent before their messages are imported
- Developers can connect agents and build their own integrations

**📚 Public documentation**
User guides and developer documentation now have a home at https://docs.voxt.ai/ — learn how to use Voxt, connect an agent, or build an integration. You can also open the help pages from Settings.

**🎙️ Push-to-talk, your way**
- A dedicated push-to-talk section in the chat list, with per-chat mute controls
- Redesigned settings make it clearer which chats and devices will answer, with a practice dialog for learning the gestures
- Pause participation without turning everything off, with more reliable listening when your phone wakes and desktop Do Not Disturb respected

**💬 More control, less friction**
- Archive chats just for yourself, mark them as read or unread, and choose which mentioned people to alert
- Manage chat history retention and transfer ownership when leaving a chat or place
- Typed messages in live conversations can be read aloud, with a listener setting to turn this off
- Share files to other apps, preview map links in chat, and edit avatars and backgrounds in a refreshed crop editor
- Smoother scrolling, more reliable video uploads, and dozens of fixes across audio, notifications, pasting and everyday chat

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!
