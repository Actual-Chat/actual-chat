**🎉 Voxt v2.14 is here! 🎉**

Sign-in now happens right inside the app, Android notifications read like a real conversation, and everything is noticeably faster and steadier.

**🔐 Sign in without leaving the app**
- Signing in and out now uses a built-in browser sheet — no more bouncing out to Chrome or Safari and back
- On Windows the app brings itself back to the front once you're done
- Sign-in problems are reported instead of failing quietly

**🔔 Notifications that read like a conversation**
- Android notifications now show a transcript of the latest messages, newest first, grouped per chat
- In group chats every message is labelled with its author

**📥 A new Unread panel**
The left panel gains an Unread group with **All**, **DMs** and **Mentions** tabs, so you can jump straight to whatever needs you.

**⚡ Faster, lighter, smoother**
- Chats open faster and load further ahead as you scroll
- No more flickering live indicator or bouncing unread badges in the chat list
- A new on-device storage engine makes the local cache quicker and lighter
- Faster startup: the splash screen goes straight into the app, with no flash in between
- Contact pickers and the chat list do far less work behind the scenes

**✍️ A tidier message editor**
Attachments, mentions and related messages now overlay the input instead of pushing it around — plus a polished send button and a better layout on mobile.

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- **Ctrl+Shift+L** toggles light and dark theme
- Expanding or collapsing a conversation no longer shifts the chat under your finger
- Headers and blank lines in messages render consistently everywhere
- Diagnostics toggles now live in your account settings
- Dozens of small fixes, plus a broad security and permissions hardening pass

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
