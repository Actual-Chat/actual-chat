**🎉 Voxt v2.13 is here! 🎉**

Live sessions get a cleaner, more polished look — plus spoiler text, keyboard shortcuts, faster mentions, and a steadier app all around.

**🗣️ Live sessions, more polished**
- A unified, cleaner live conversation block — a sticky title with a scrollable transcript below
- A **"Show earlier messages"** pill lets you peek back through a live session without losing your place
- Start banners name who's talking, and sessions close smoothly once everyone's done
- Copy a live conversation straight from its footer

**🙈 Spoiler text**
Hide surprises with `||spoiler||` — the text stays masked until someone taps to reveal it.

**😀 Emoji mentions & a faster mention picker**
Mention with emoji, and the mention popup now appears instantly as you type.

**⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts**
Press **Ctrl+/** (**Cmd+/** on Mac) for a shortcuts cheat sheet, and use **PageUp/PageDown** to scroll the chat even while writing a message.

**✨ Steadier & smoother**
- More reliable video streaming when the network hiccups
- Fewer crashes and quicker startup on Android
- Faster connections with compression across chat
- Plus dozens of small fixes across chat, scrolling, and notifications

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!
