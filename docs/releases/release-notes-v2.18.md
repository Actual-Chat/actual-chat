**🎉 Voxt v2.18 is here! 🎉**

Inviting friends is now a QR scan away, Voxt learned to talk in your car, typing indicators arrived, and video streams got a serious reliability pass on every platform.

**📲 Invite friends with a QR code**
- Every link Voxt shares — your profile, a chat invite, a place — now has a Share / Copy / QR row, so you can show a code instead of typing anything
- Scan a friend's code with the in-app camera and they land straight in your contacts or the add-members list
- New accounts get a friendly "invite your friends" nudge in the chat list

**🚗 Android Auto**
Voxt now knows when your phone is projecting to the car: choose whether playback goes to the car speakers or stays on the phone, and keep recording from the phone mic while you drive. There's a new Car Audio tab in Settings for it.

**⌨️ Typing indicators**
See who's typing in the chat header, the chat list and the members panel — and it stops the moment they do.

**🔔 Notifications & reactions**
- The notifications panel gained a Reactions tab, chats with new reactions float to the top, and chat rows show the reaction emoji
- iOS push banners are now titled with the chat, not the sender, and Android draws chat avatars natively
- Banners under the chat header were redesigned and now collapse into a neat stack
- Notifications you've dealt with elsewhere clear more reliably on every device

**⚙️ Settings, sorted**
- A new Permissions tab gathers every microphone, camera and notification permission in one place, with platform-specific guidance — the old banners are gone
- The time zone editor was rebuilt with search, your detected zone, and city/offset views
- Settings tabs were reorganized; the back button on narrow screens now returns to the tab list

**📹 Video streams that hold up**
- Video streams negotiate the best codec everyone can decode, Firefox is now fully supported, and five remote tiles fit on screen sorted by who's speaking
- A long list of audio fixes: silent stalls, headsets plugged in mid-recording, the iOS loudspeaker, Windows echo cancellation, Android capture recovery
- Sharper self-preview and steadier playback under changing bandwidth

**💬 In the chat**
- "Download all" for a message's attachments, saved as one action
- Long-press opens the message menu instead of selecting text; selecting transcript text no longer starts playback
- Block quotes nest properly, code fences honor their language, and http:// deep links open the app on Android
- The live conversation card keeps ten recent messages under it and reveals twenty more per tap, without the list blinking
- Chats you open often are prefetched so they open even offline

**⚡ Faster and lighter**
Android starts faster, especially when woken by a push; the Windows app starts faster too; and the web bundle shed several hundred kilobytes of fonts and icons.

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- The new-chat button morphs into an X while its menu is open, menus scale in, and buttons got a subtle sheen
- The start screen and sign-in are fully localized
- iOS share sheet shows contact skeletons while it loads
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across scrolling, safe areas, modals and the chat list

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
