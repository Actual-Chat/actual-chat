**🎉 Voxt v2.16 is here! 🎉**

Pinned messages, a new Unread panel, hashtags — and a chat that finally scrolls the way it should.

**📌 Pinned messages**
Pin messages in any chat. Pinned ones sit in a bar at the top — tap to jump to one, or forward and unpin it from the bar's menu.

**🔔 A new Unread panel**
Opens from the logo menu and replaces the old notifications feed, with a red dot on the bell when there's something new. Chats you've read stay in the panel until you close it, and a new setting controls how long items stick around.

**#️⃣ Hashtags and tables**
Type `#something` and it becomes a link — tap it to search your messages for that tag. Messages now render Markdown-style tables too.

**📍 Sharing a place**
- Send any place you pick on the map, not just where you happen to be
- "Send my current location" is more precise, and shows an accuracy circle
- Open a shared location in whichever map app you have installed
- A clear prompt instead of a blank map when location permission is off

**🎧 Listening and transcription**
- One setting now controls how long Voxt keeps listening after someone stops talking — Off, 5, 10 or 30 seconds — replacing the old per-chat modes
- Transcription is more accurate: a new engine, automatic fallback when one provider struggles, and chat context to get names and terms right
- Calmer cues — a short vibration or beep — and Voxt only reminds you it's recording when nobody's talking

**📜 A chat that stays put**
The message list has been rebuilt. Scrolling no longer jumps or snaps back, conversation headers stay anchored, the overscroll bounce feels natural, and live conversations no longer blank out or hide messages typed during a call.

**⚡ Faster and lighter**
Messages parse about twice as fast, startup is quicker, and there's less battery drain and heat during calls and long sessions. On iOS, the share extension reaches a usable contact list far faster.

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- A new Moderator role in chats and places, alongside Owner
- Share a chat or a place straight from its menu
- iPhone audio stays on the right output when you plug in headphones or get in the car
- Android's recording banner no longer covers the app
- Rename someone and the new name shows up everywhere they're mentioned
- Dozens of small fixes across uploads, the gallery, invites, avatars and downloads

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!
