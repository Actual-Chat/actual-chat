**🎉 Voxt v2.15 is here! 🎉**

Live location sharing is now open to everyone, attachments finally save where you expect them to, and the Android app is a much smaller download.

**📍 Share your live location — now for everyone**
- Sharing your live location in a chat is no longer limited: it's available to all users, on every platform
- A new activity pill sits above the chat when something's live, so you're always one tap away from the map — and one tap away from stopping the share
- The stop-sharing button now lives right in the chat's activity panel, next to the map
- Re-sharing your location properly replaces the previous share instead of leaving it running

**📎 Attachments that actually save**
- **Download now saves the file** instead of handing it off to your browser. On Android it lands in Downloads, Music or your gallery; on iOS and Mac it goes to Photos or through the share sheet to Files
- File attachments get their own menu, with **Preview** alongside Download
- PDFs can be previewed right in the viewer

**📦 A lighter Android app**
The Android download is about 20 MB smaller — we stripped out images the app never showed and debug data nothing reads at runtime. Same app, less to download and less space used on your phone.

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- Android notification banners now show which chat a message came from
- The Threads tab says "No threads yet" instead of sitting empty
- The share sheet on iOS no longer stalls
- The new-chat button no longer hides behind the home indicator on phones
- A number of small fixes and polish across the UI

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
