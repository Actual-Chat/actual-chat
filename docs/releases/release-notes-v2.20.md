**🎉 Voxt v2.20 is here! 🎉**

Android Auto gets a real audio setup, and the chat view got a deep round of polish — from live blocks to scrolling to photos on Mac.

**🚗 Android Auto**
- One three-way audio choice on the Android Auto tab — Car, Phone or Auto — with Car as the default
- Speech is boosted on the car speakers, playback keeps its place when the audio route changes, and the car mic is only opened when you actually chose it

**💬 Chat view**
- A new button takes you straight to unread reactions
- Live conversation blocks fold and reveal more predictably: collapsing keeps just the card, pending messages stay inside the block after you leave, and nothing gets lost when the block dissolves
- Sending a message no longer reloads the loaded window, and new items animate in at the pinned edge
- Trackpad scrolling to the end pins properly, and chat switching on iPhone/iPad no longer stalls on stale content
- Search finds system chats by their localized title
- Mentioning yourself in a chat you haven't joined no longer breaks the chat
- Messages render with a lighter footprint, so long chats scroll smoother

**🔔 Notifications**
- Group message banners are headlined with the chat, not the sender
- Reaction notifications say who reacted, and coalesced banners keep the chat's order
- Mac notification banners show the chat icon

**🖼️ Attachments & photos**
- Mac: photos and videos are picked from the Photos app, and file pickers open from inside the app
- Picked attachments are added together instead of one after another, and empty files are refused up front
- The avatar crop dialog shows progress while it saves

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- Flat, themed inputs across all modals and the share dialog, and a fixed double safe-area gap under modal buttons
- Mac: the window's traffic lights are clickable and closing hides the window
- Windows: the Quit jump-list icon renders again
- Video: tiles recover after a reconnect, and an idle screencast no longer restarts itself
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across translation, transcription, uploads, connectivity and the chat list

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
