**🎉 Voxt v2.21 is here! 🎉**

A big one: hear translations spoken as people talk, passkey sign-in, and push-to-talk for everyone — plus notification history and a fresh look across the app.

**🌍 Translated voice (alpha)**
- Turn on "Hear translations spoken" and Voxt voices the translation over the original as people talk — in near real time
- Pick a voice with previews; replays play the translated voice too
- Live translation now works in shorter phrases, so it keeps up with the speaker
- Live translation is an alpha-quality feature — expect rough edges, and please tell us what you hear

**🎙️ Push-to-talk for everyone**
- Push-to-talk is now available to all users
- Pocket detection only stops the mic when it should — never on the earpiece, a headset or in the car
- Android Auto: talk to the car assistant, and the phone mic is used for better pickup

**🔐 Passkeys**
- Sign in with a passkey on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, and manage them in Settings

**🔔 Notifications & email**
- The bell now keeps a 30-day history below your active notifications
- A daily email digest of unread chats on days you haven't opened the app — with its own Settings panel, delivery time and one-click unsubscribe
- The app icon badge counts the same way everywhere

**🖼️ Photos & media**
- iPhone HEIC photos now display everywhere, not just in Safari
- Choose upload quality for images, with instant placeholders while they load
- AI-suggested pictures and backgrounds for chats and places
- The apps cache media on disk, so chats you've seen load faster

**🎨 Look & feel**
- New typography with larger, more readable text, and unified dialogs that stay put when the keyboard opens
- Themed, animated kitty illustrations
- Quote: reply with just the text you selected
- Mac: the app extends under the title bar and you can drag the window by the panel headers; Windows: the title bar follows your theme
- A friendly rate-the-app prompt after a live session

**⚡ Under the hood**
- Faster Android startup and lighter audio capture
- Replays play in the right order and no longer drift after pauses
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across sign-in, onboarding, audio, threads and the chat list

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
