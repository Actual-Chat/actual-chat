**🎉 Voxt v2.17 is here! 🎉**

Voxt now speaks your language — this release brings a fully localized app, a redesigned live conversation view, better notifications, and steadier calls.

**🌍 Voxt in your language**
- The whole app is now localized — UI, dates, error messages, even push notifications arrive in your language
- Eight new languages join the family: Polish, Czech, Bulgarian, Indonesian, Bosnian, Croatian, Montenegrin and Serbian
- Pick your UI language in Settings — it follows your account to every device, and the native apps detect your system language properly again
- Cleaner, shorter date and time formats everywhere

**🎙️ Live conversations, redesigned**
The live conversation block got a fresh look, with a subtle "fog" showing where listening ends and history begins. While you're following the conversation at the bottom, unread badges stay quiet — and the "show more" pill now behaves under the pinned header.

**🔔 Better notifications**
- Chat icons show up in push notifications on iOS, and group chats get proper avatars
- Notifications are cleaned up more reliably across devices once you've dealt with them
- Faster cold start when a push wakes the Android app

**📞 Steadier calls**
A long list of stability fixes for video calls: cameras no longer end up sideways on iOS, self-preview works on all browsers, audio and video stay in sync, and the player recovers gracefully from decoder hiccups instead of freezing.

**⚡ Faster and lighter**
Chats you use most are preloaded before you open them, tapping a chat starts loading it instantly, the app postpones invisible work while backgrounded to save battery, and the Windows and Android apps start faster.

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- Sign-in codes fall back to Telegram when SMS can't reach you
- #hashtag search now finds exact tags, not just similar words
- Drop files straight onto the Windows app
- Bluetooth audio stays in music quality on Android instead of dropping to call quality
- When your mic fails, Voxt tells you what's actually wrong
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across gestures, modals, safe areas and animations

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
