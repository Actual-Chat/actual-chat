**🎉 Voxt v2.19 is here! 🎉**

Search can now find your own messages, Voxt speaks your language in a lot more places, attention pings stand out, and the phone experience got noticeably smoother.

**🔍 Search "My messages"**
A new result type in search shows only the messages you wrote, combinable with the Anywhere / Chat / Place filters.

**🌍 More of Voxt in your language**
- Native dialogs, notifications and the iOS Live Activity are now localized
- Location sharing durations, distances, countdowns and map types, the Notes and Announcements chats, and the system permission prompts on iOS and Mac follow your language too
- The app's UI language now matches the language iOS uses for its own prompts

**🔔 Notifications & mentions**
- Attention pings show up as a red "@" in the chat list and on the Mentions tab, first in line, even in muted chats — and they're delivered even when you're already caught up
- Reaction notifications name who reacted in group chats, show the actual emoji, and clear when you've actually seen them; removing a reaction no longer notifies
- iOS banners are titled with the sender again
- Share just the link from a link preview, not the whole message

**🤝 Smoother on the phone**
- The side panel follows your finger with real momentum, opens on a quick flick, and no longer freezes mid-slide on Android
- iPhones with ProMotion displays now render Voxt above 60 fps
- Chats you've opened before render offline instead of waiting for the network
- The first "@" mention list in a chat opens instantly
- Scrolling got a deep pass: no more jumps while messages animate in, the date visor tracks the list properly, and the first-unread jump lands where it should
- Video calls and the call footer draw less when idle, easing battery use

**🎨 Small things you might like**
- Refreshed button states, a wider back-button tap zone, share / reply / forward icons, and rounded wallpaper corners across panels
- GIFs load with a skeleton, the settings tiles read well in dark mode on mobile, and the splash screen no longer shifts the layout
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across attachments, safe areas, side panels and the chat list

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
