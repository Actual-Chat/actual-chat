**🎉 Voxt v2.23 is here! 🎉**

Calls get friendlier and harder to miss, Voxt speaks 12 more languages, and our cats got a fresh, animated makeover.

**📞 Better calls**
- Raised hands are hard to miss now: a gentle sound for hosts and speakers, a waving hand badge, and a counter of raised hands in the call header
- See your camera preview while the call is still ringing, and get notified when the other person turns their video on mid-call
- Choose whether to transcribe the call right from the dialing screen
- A bigger record button that clearly shows when you're muted, plus a hint on how to hang up the first time you mute
- Reactions stay within reach in full-screen and while muted

**🌍 Voxt in 12 more languages**
The app is now available in Danish, Dutch, Filipino, Finnish, Kazakh, Malay, Marathi, Punjabi, Swedish, Tamil, Thai and Uzbek — 34 languages in all. Your chosen app language is also restored reliably on startup.

**🎙️ Push-to-talk at a glance**
- Push-to-talk chats now sit in one compact row with their avatars, showing who's talking and where you're recording
- Push-to-talk chats appear in Active Chats while someone's talking, and the row stays in place while you search
- Dismiss the "device off" banner and enjoy a reworked push-to-talk chat dialog

**🐱 A fresh look**
- Redesigned cat illustrations for loading, empty chats, search, invites, errors, sharing and more — many now animated, all tuned for light and dark themes
- Loading skeletons on every side-panel tab, refreshed settings icons, and matching mention chips in the editor and messages

**🤖 For AI agents**
Agents connected over MCP can now wait for new messages and unread chats, and archive chats.

**🛠️ Smoother everyday use**
- Videos show a sharp, centered preview while they load and play without a flicker
- Sharing large videos from iPhone is more reliable
- Contact names prefer the matching entry in your address book
- Notifications for deleted messages disappear on their own
- Windows: echo cancellation works with any speaker or headset, and the app installs its runtime itself
- Android: clearer audio while your microphone is on
- Dozens of small fixes — quiet polish across calls, scrolling, translation and startup speed

Thanks for being part of the Voxt community — keep the feedback coming!

— The Voxt.ai team 🎧
